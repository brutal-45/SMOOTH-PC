
## - For Portraits & Faces:

 Restoration and enhancement of facial details: natural skin texture, clear eyes, defined hair strands, and soft directional lighting. Remove blur, correct skin tones, and enhance micro-expressions while keeping features completely natural and realistic.

## - For Landscapes & Architecture:

Enhance image clarity and depth. Boost dynamic range, saturate natural vibrant colors, balance shadow/highlight details, crisp sharpness on textures (rock, foliage, glass), 8K detail, golden hour natural lighting.

## - For Vintage / Old / Low-Res Photos:

Restore and enhance damaged low-resolution vintage photograph: de-noise, de-blur, repair scratches, upscale resolution, enhance clarity, restore natural colors, sharp focus, 4k photorealistic detail.
