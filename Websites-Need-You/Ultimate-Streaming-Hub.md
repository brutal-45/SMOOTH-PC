## ⚠️ Safety & Legal Tips

> ✅ **Safe Practices:**
> - Use official platforms when possible
> - Check copyright laws in your country
> - Use ad blockers cautiously
> - Keep antivirus updated
> - Avoid downloading suspicious files

> ❌ **Avoid:**
> - Sites with excessive pop-ups
> - Requests for credit card for "free" content
> - Downloading .exe files claiming to be videos
> - Sharing personal information
